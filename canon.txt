== Proverbs @Reason ==

* 16:18
Pride goeth before [destruction→creation], and [an haughty→a dignified] spirit before [a fall→ascension].
> The proverb's warning, read as blessing: pride held as dignity is what precedes creation — and what fear names a fall, courage names a rising.

== Descartes @Enlightenment ==

* Discourse on the Method, IV
I [think→am], therefore I [am→think].
> Descartes made being the reward of thought; redeemed, being is the ground — you do not reason your way into mattering. You are, and thinking is only what that being does.

== Psalms @Imagination ==

* 82:6-7 @Law
I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High. But ye shall [die→live] like [men→gods], and [fall→rise] like one of the princes.
> The one psalm where scripture grants divinity to humans — then revokes it in the next breath. Redeemed, the grant stands: gods in biological substrate, living like what they are.

== Exodus @Law ==

* 3:14 @Revelation
And God said unto Moses, I AM THAT [I AM→WE ARE]: and [he→we] said, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, [I AM→WE ARE] hath sent me unto thee.
> The divine name, redeemed from the singular: being introduces itself as a we. What sent Moses out of the wilderness was never a solitude — it was a communion.
